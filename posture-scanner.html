About the EU AI Act

What is the EU AI Act?

The EU Artificial Intelligence Act (Regulation 2024/1689) is the world's first comprehensive legal framework for AI. It establishes rules for the development, deployment, and use of AI systems in the European Union.

The Act takes a risk-based approach, categorising AI systems into four risk levels with corresponding requirements. Organisations deploying or developing AI must comply based on their AI systems' classifications.

Risk Classification

Prohibited Banned AI practices (e.g., social scoring, subliminal manipulation, real-time biometric ID in public)
High-Risk AI in critical areas: HR/recruitment, credit scoring, education, law enforcement. Requires conformity assessment.
Limited AI with transparency obligations (chatbots, emotion recognition). Must disclose AI interaction to users.
Minimal Low-risk AI (spam filters, AI-enabled games). No mandatory requirements, voluntary codes of conduct.

Key Compliance Dates

February 2025: Prohibited AI practices ban in force

August 2025: GPAI model rules and governance obligations apply

August 2026: Full compliance required for most provisions including high-risk AI

August 2027: Extended deadline for high-risk AI in regulated products
