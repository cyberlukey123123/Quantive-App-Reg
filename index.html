EU AI Act Risk Categories

The EU AI Act classifies AI systems into four risk categories, each with different compliance requirements.

Prohibited AI

AI systems that pose unacceptable risks, including social scoring, real-time biometric identification, and manipulative AI. These are banned outright.

High-Risk AI

AI used in critical areas like employment, education, law enforcement, and essential services. Requires conformity assessments, human oversight, and documentation.

Limited Risk AI

AI systems like chatbots and content generators that require transparency obligations. Users must be informed they are interacting with AI.

Minimal Risk AI

Low-risk AI applications like spam filters and inventory management. No specific obligations but voluntary codes of conduct are encouraged.
