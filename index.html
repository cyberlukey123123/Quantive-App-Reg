EU AI Act Risk Classification

Our scanner also identifies discovered AI systems and classifies them according to EU AI Act risk categories. This helps you understand regulatory obligations alongside your technical readiness.

Prohibited AI

Banned AI systems including social scoring and real-time biometric identification. Must be removed immediately.

High-Risk AI

HR/recruitment AI, credit scoring. Requires conformity assessment, human oversight, and documentation.

Limited Risk AI

Chatbots, AI assistants like Copilot. Must disclose AI use to users with transparency notices.

Minimal Risk AI

Spam filters, productivity AI. No mandatory requirements - voluntary best practices apply.
